# Hi, I'm Arkar Than Win

I am a PhD candidate at the Institute of Transport and Logistics Studies, specialising in transport planning and modelling. This GitHub profile showcases my research outputs, including publications, presentations, posters, and other teaching materials. 

## Research interests

- Smart mobility
- Transport planning
- Travel behaviour
- Accessibility
- Transport-related social exclusion
- Transport infrastructure
- Transport policy and governance
